# From Data to Strategy: Building Operational Intelligence for Qantas

## Overview

This project analyzes operational reliability within the Australian aviation industry, with a focus on Qantas and its major competitors, Virgin Australia and Jetstar. Using historical airline performance data, the project explores trends in on-time performance, delays, cancellations, and route reliability to support strategic decision-making.

The project was developed as a Business Intelligence and Data Visualization project using Tableau to transform raw aviation data into actionable insights for executive stakeholders.

---

## Business Problem

Operational reliability plays a critical role in customer satisfaction, airline reputation, and overall business performance. Delays and cancellations can disrupt airline operations, impact passengers, and increase operational costs.

The goal of this project is to help decision-makers understand:

* Long-term reliability trends
* Competitive performance within the Australian aviation market
* High-impact operational routes
* Delay and cancellation patterns
* Seasonal operational challenges

---

## Dataset

The analysis uses the Australian Domestic Airline On-Time Performance dataset published by the Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE).

### Key Metrics

* On-Time Performance
* Delay Rates

* Route Performance
* Airline Comparisons

## Tableau Dashboard

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of operational reliability across Australian airlines.

### Dashboard Components

### Operational Reliability Trends (2004–2026)

Visualizes long-term changes in on-time performance and allows comparison between major airlines.

### High-Volume Route Analysis (2025)

Highlights the busiest Qantas routes and their operational significance.

### Delay vs Cancellation Analysis

Examines the relationship between delays and cancellations to identify operational risk areas.

### Seasonal Delay Patterns (2025)

Shows monthly delay trends to support planning and resource allocation.

---

## Key Insights

### Competitive Performance Analysis

Qantas' operational reliability was evaluated against major Australian competitors to understand its market position.

### Route-Level Operational Impact

High-volume routes were identified as critical areas where disruptions can significantly affect network performance.

### Reliability Risk Assessment

The relationship between delays and cancellations highlighted potential operational vulnerabilities.

### Seasonal Trends

Certain periods showed higher delay rates, indicating opportunities for improved operational planning.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Strengthen operational planning during high-risk periods.
* Prioritize performance improvements on high-volume routes.
* Monitor delay and cancellation patterns proactively.
* Use competitor benchmarking to identify improvement opportunities.
* Allocate resources strategically based on seasonal demand patterns.
